1/**
2 * @file clientcontroller.hpp
3 * @brief Client-specific handler that layers password hashing, sessions and
4 * account state over the generic CRUDS flow.
5 * @details Declares `ClientController<T>`, which inherits storage from
6 * EntityController<T> and the account contract from ClientControllerBase.
7 * Simple operations (Read/Search) just forward to the base. Create/Update are
8 * special: they run on a PasswordWorker because hashing is
9 * deliberately slow. Login/Logout and account state go through GateKeeper. The
10 * definitions and instantiations live in clientcontroller.cpp.
11 *
12 * WHY THIS FILE MATTERS IN THE SERVER:
13 * Accounts need protections that ordinary records do not: passwords must be
14 * hashed off the request thread, and sessions must be invalidated when an
15 * account is deleted or suspended. This class adds exactly those behaviours on
16 * top of the generic entity pipeline. Without it, clients would be treated like
17 * any other row and password hashing would block the server's IO threads.
18 *
19 * CONCEPTS TO LEARN (for beginners):
20 * - Multiple inheritance:
21 * https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/derived_class
22 * - `final` overrides (cannot be overridden further):
23 * https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/virtual
24 * - Lambdas captured by value for worker jobs:
25 * https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/lambda
26 * - Coroutines (co_await / drogon::Task):
27 * https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/coroutines
28 * - Concepts / constrained templates (`Client_t`):
29 * https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/constraints
30 * - std::shared_ptr (sharing services):
31 * https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/memory/shared_ptr
32 *
33 * @author Amr Osman <dr3mro@gmail.com>
34 * @date 2026-11-16
35 */

64/**
65 * @class ClientController
66 * @brief Client-specific handler that layers password hashing, sessions and
67 * account state over the generic CRUDS flow.
68 * @details Inherits persistence from EntityController<T> and the full client
69 * contract from ClientControllerBase. Read/Search and their coroutine forms
70 * simply forward to EntityController; Create/Update dispatch their bodies to a
71 * PasswordWorker so password hashing never occupies a Drogon IO thread.
72 * Login/Logout are delegated to GateKeeper, and Suspend/Delete additionally
73 * invalidate sessions on success.
74 * @tparam T Entity type satisfying `Client_t` (derives from `api::v2::Client`),
75 * e.g. User or Provider.
76 * @note All overrides are `final`. Coroutine variants borrow their arguments
77 * and must be awaited while they live.
78 * @see ClientControllerBase, EntityController, PasswordWorker
79 */
